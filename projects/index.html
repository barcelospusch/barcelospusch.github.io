01

Citizen science · Small Solar System bodies

Asteroid hunting
campaigns.

As founder-president and mentor of the Núcleo de Pesquisa e Caça de Asteroides (NPCA), I organize teams for the NPCA Asteroid Search Campaign. The group is affiliated with the International Astronomical Search Collaboration (IASC) and brings together members of the public and students from Colégio Estadual Professor Loureiro Fernandes.

The campaign is a practical way to engage with the search for asteroids: participants work collaboratively with astronomical observations and learn how careful, repeatable analysis can contribute to the study of objects in our Solar System. Through mentoring, I support teams as they build confidence with scientific observation, evidence, and shared investigation.

02

Observational astronomy · Research

Stellar
occultations.

Through a scientific initiation scholarship with PPGFA-UTFPR, under the guidance of Prof. Dr. F. Braga-Ribas, I contribute to research on stellar occultations. In an occultation, a Solar System body passes in front of a distant star from the observer's point of view; observations of the event offer a way to investigate the foreground body's properties.

My work focuses on analysing astrometric and photometric data for the mapping and physical characterization of small Solar System bodies. This combines precise measurements with observational context, contributing to the work of the observational astronomy group and to a broader understanding of these objects.

PPGFA · UTFPR

03

Science outreach · Astronomy & astronautics

Making room
for discovery.

Alongside research, I take part in academic activities and events focused on astronomy, astronautics, citizen science, and science outreach for young people. As a member of the Ciências Espaciais Longe Lateqve Team (CEELL-OACEP), I help connect space science with student communities.

I am interested in creating approachable ways for young people to encounter scientific ideas, ask questions, and see themselves as participants in discovery. These interests also include astrophysics and rocket physics.

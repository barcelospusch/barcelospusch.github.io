Dimitri Barcelos Pusch

High school student at Colégio Estadual do Paraná. He is a member of the Ciências Espaciais Longe Lateqve Team (CEELL-OACEP), serves as president and mentor of the Núcleo de Pesquisa e Caça de Asteroides, and holds a scientific initiation scholarship in stellar occultations through PPGFA-UTFPR. He participates in academic activities and events focused on astronomy, astronautics, citizen science, and science outreach for youth. His interests include rocket physics, astrophysics, and astronomy.

Research Interests

Asteroid Hunting

As the founder-president and mentor of the Núcleo de Pesquisa e Caça de Asteroides (NPCA)—a research group affiliated with the International Astronomical Search Collaboration—he organizes teams for the NPCA Asteroid Search Campaign, involving both the general public and students from Colégio Estadual Professor Loureiro Fernandes.

NPCA
Undergraduate Research Scholarship Recipient in Stellar Occultations

As a scholarship recipient for a scientific initiation project focused on the study and observation of stellar occultations—under the guidance of Prof. Dr. F. Braga-Ribas—he works directly on the analysis of astrometric and photometric data for the mapping and physical characterization of small Solar System bodies, actively contributing to research conducted by the observational astronomy group.
